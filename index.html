Where the questions and answers came from

Questions come from the Exams folder. Chapter placement and the answers to unmarked papers come from the slides folder. Every question shows one of these labels once you answer it:

  • OFFICIAL KEY The answer key printed in the exam file (model answer).
  • GRADED PAPER A student's paper that the instructor marked correct (or marked wrong, so the other answer is right).
  • MARKED + CHECKED An answer selected on a quiz screenshot or boxed on the paper, which I checked against the slides.
  • FROM SLIDES The paper has no answer; the answer comes from the chapter slides.
  • WORKED OUT The paper has no answer; I worked it out from the tables or diagram printed in the question.
